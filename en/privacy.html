Equalize Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

1. Overview

Equalize is a puzzle game published by an independent developer. No account is required. This notice explains the information processed by the app and the third-party services included in it.

2. Data stored on your device

Game progress, stars, best results, hint balance, Pro entitlement and settings are stored locally on your device. Your operating system may include this data in device backups. Removing the app normally removes its local data, subject to your backup settings.

3. Diagnostics, analytics and advertising

The app includes Firebase Crashlytics and Firebase Analytics to diagnose crashes and understand app reliability and feature usage. Usage Analytics is disabled by default and can be changed in Settings. Firebase Remote Config retrieves the optional More Games catalog and may use a Firebase installation identifier. The app also includes Google Mobile Ads for advertising features. Depending on platform settings, region and consent choices, these services may process device or app identifiers, advertising identifiers, app interactions, coarse technical information and crash diagnostics. Google acts under its own privacy terms.

4. Purchases and external links

Apple or Google processes store purchases and payment information; Equalize does not receive your full payment-card details. Links to support pages or other games open outside the app and are governed by the destination service. The first release validates the native store transaction on device; authoritative receipt, refund and revocation validation requires a future server-side service.

5. Use, sharing and retention

Information is used to operate the game, preserve settings, diagnose failures, measure performance, prevent abuse and provide enabled ad features. It is shared only with service providers needed for those purposes or when legally required. Provider retention periods are controlled by the applicable Firebase, Google Ads and store settings.

6. Children and your choices

The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children contrary to applicable law. Platform privacy and advertising settings may provide additional controls. Contact us to ask a privacy question or request action concerning data associated with the app.

7. Changes

This policy may change when the app or its service providers change. The effective date above identifies the current in-app version.
